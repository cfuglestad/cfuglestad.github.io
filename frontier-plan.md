## SOL Recommendations (verbatim)

### 1. Replace placeholder projects with substantive case studies

Replace "in development" project cards with 2–3 substantive case studies showing the problem, healthcare or NLP relevance, methods, architecture, validation, measurable outcomes, limitations, and links to code/demo/write-up. Hiring managers and research PIs need proof of rigor more than decorative refinement.

### 2. Sharpen positioning in hero and About copy

Rewrite the hero and About copy around a differentiated value proposition: healthcare domain understanding, production analytics engineering, and scientifically responsible AI/NLP. State target collaborations or roles clearly. The current framing is credible but broad.

### 3. Make professional impact scannable with quantified achievements

Add quantified achievements to every recent experience entry: scale, adoption, reliability, time saved, cost, team leadership, or decision impact. Conference organizers and hiring leaders should understand seniority within seconds.

### 4. Strengthen trust signals

Add selected publications, talks, technical writing, open-source work, certifications, awards, and professional affiliations where truthful. Include a polished downloadable résumé and clear LinkedIn/GitHub links. Research-facing audiences look for externally verifiable credibility.

### 5. Improve information architecture

Give major projects dedicated detail pages and make the résumé a complete, polished page rather than a placeholder. Preserve the homepage as an executive summary. This supports both rapid screening and deeper technical evaluation.

### 6. Complete responsive accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Add an accessible mobile menu, visible keyboard focus, skip link, semantic landmarks, reduced-motion support, sufficient footer contrast, and tested heading/link behavior. Professional quality is undermined immediately by inaccessible navigation.

### 7. Restrained visual refinement

Establish a deliberate typography system with actually loaded, performant fonts; improve card hierarchy and hover/focus states; add subtle motion only where it communicates state. Dark mode is lower priority than typography, spacing, and interaction consistency.

### 8. Finish discoverability and resilience

Add canonical, Open Graph, Twitter card, social preview image, per-page metadata, a useful 404 page, sitemap/robots verification, and Lighthouse testing. These determine how credible the portfolio appears when shared by recruiters or conference committees.
